# Burmese Lore, Folklore & Satirical Fiction Catalog

This reference catalog provides authentic narrative and mechanical roots from Myanmar classical folklore, trickster tales, historical legends, and vintage satirical comics for game design in *Phoe Htaung*.

---

## 1. Folkloric Tricksters & Ancient Wit

### 1.1 Wungyi U Paw U (ဝန်ကြီးဦးပေါ်ဦး လျှောက်ထုံးများ)
* **Archetype:** The Supreme Royal Wit / Lateral Thinking Strategist.
* **Core Philosophy:** Overcoming impossible despotic royal edicts through brilliant linguistic subversion, everyday folk physics, and comedic misdirection.
* **Key Tales & Puzzle Applications:**
  - **Weighing the Royal White Elephant (ဆင်ဖြူတော်ကို ချိန်တွယ်ခြင်း):**
    - *Mechanic:* Water displacement. Putting the heavy object onto a boat, marking the waterline with chalk, removing the object, and filling the boat with stones until the waterline matches.
    - *Game Puzzle:* Balancing scales or flooding chambers using weight substitution (e.g. stones, water pots, Phoe Htaung's own body weight).
  - **The Red Betel Nut vs. The Royal Ruby (ကွမ်းသီးနီနှင့် ပတ္တမြားငမောက်):**
    - *Mechanic:* Seductive bait & color camouflage. Replacing an ancient gem with a carved betel nut soaked in lime and catechu to fool greedy sentries or pressure-sensitive pedestals.
  - **The Measure of a Straight Line (မျဉ်းဖြောင့်ကို မဖြတ်ဘဲ တိုစေခြင်း):**
    - *Mechanic:* Relativity. Making a line shorter without touching it by drawing a longer line next to it.
    - *Game Puzzle:* Extending a bridge or bypassing a barrier by moving the frame of reference or environmental perspective rather than destroying the obstacle.

### 1.2 Maung Pauk Kyaing (မောင်ပေါက်ကျိုင်းနှင့် တက္ကသိုလ်ပညာ)
* **Archetype:** The Diligent Underdog / Riddle Solver.
* **Core Philosophy:** Surviving lethal supernatural trials through nocturnal vigilance, following cryptic elder advice, and environmental observation.
* **Key Tales & Puzzle Applications:**
  - **The Four Golden Maxims (ပညာရှိစကား လေးခွန်း):**
    - *"မအိပ်မနေ အသက်ရှည်"* (He who stays awake survives): Traps with sleeping or periodic dormancy cycles. If Phoe Htaung stands still, danger approaches; active movement or night-watch tools keep hazards at bay.
    - *"မေးခါထုတ်ထုတ် သက်ငယ်စုတ်"* (Questioning thoroughly saves life): Probing mysterious statues, switches, or alcoves using <kbd>E</kbd> or poking with a bamboo pole before stepping into danger.
    - *"သွားခါထုတ်ထုတ် ခြေထောက်တို"* (Frequent travel shortens distance): Finding shortcuts, secret crawl tunnels (<kbd>S</kbd>), or wall-press passages.
    - *"မဆုတ်မနစ် စိတ်ကိုမွေး"* (Perseverance conquers all): Overcoming high-difficulty athletic platforming segments.
  - **The Dragon Slaying by Substitution (နဂါးမင်းကို နှိမ်နင်းခြင်း):**
    - *Mechanic:* Placing a banana tree trunk dressed in royal blankets into bed. The dragon strikes the fake dummy, gets stuck with sticky sap, and is easily defeated.
    - *Game Trap:* Baiting a crushing ceiling hazard or rolling boulder into a decoy prop (e.g., stuffed Shan bag or wooden barrel).

### 1.3 Nga Htet Pya (ဓားပြဗိုလ် ငထက်ပြား)
* **Archetype:** The Robin Hood Trickster / Agile Infiltrator.
* **Core Philosophy:** Mocking wealthy oppressors, impossible physical escapes, and theatrical disguises.
* **Key Puzzle Applications:**
  - **Sesame Oil Wall Climb (နှမ်းဆီလူးနံရံ):** Walls coated in grease where upright climbing slips, requiring chalk/ash powder or belly-sliding beneath the hazard.
  - **Smoke & Cinnabar Distraction (ဆေးခိုးငွေ့):** Burning dried betel leaves or herbal powder to blind mechanical watchers, sentry gargoyles, or poison dart launchers.
  - **The Phantom Footprints:** Walking backwards into mud or wet lime to deceive chase sequences or pressure-pad sensors.

## 2. Supernatural Entities & Mystic Lore

### 2.1 Zawgyi (ဇော်ဂျီ) & Alchemy (ထွက်ရပ်ပေါက်/အောက်လမ်းပညာ)
* **Archetype:** The Immortal Forest Sorcerer / Herbal Chemist.
* **Visuals & Props:** Red pointed cap, red robe, flying wooden staff (*တောင်ဝှေး*), magical grinding stone (*ကျောက်ပြင်*).
* **Key Puzzle Applications:**
  - **The Magic Staff Vault:** Using an elongated bamboo pole or Zawgyi staff to pole-vault over pits, tripwires, or crushing rollers.
  - **Mercury & Sulfur Transmutation:** Combining elemental items inside Phoe Htaung's Shan bag (*Lwe-Ait*) to melt iron padlocks or dissolve ancient resin glue.
  - **Sleep Herb (ဆေးမြစ်):** Throwing wild medicinal roots into bubbling pots or boiling water to put guard animals (or clockwork guardians) into standby mode.

### 2.2 Nat Spirits & Bo Bo Gyi (နတ်ကြီးများနှင့် ဘိုးဘိုးကြီး)
* **Archetype:** Sacred Guardians of Relics and Subterranean Treasures (*သိုက်သမိုင်း*).
* **Visuals & Props:** Pointing stone statue finger, ancient bronze gongs, betel offerings, sweet banana bunches.
* **Key Puzzle Applications:**
  - **The Offerings Balance:** A stone Nat will only open the golden gate if offered something traditional: spitting fresh red betel juice (<kbd>E</kbd>) onto the dried offering bowl, or placing an authentic ripe banana into the stone palm.
  - **The Pointing Finger Riddle (ဘိုးဘိုးကြီး လက်ညှိုးညွှန်ရာ):** The guardian statue's outstretched finger points not to the treasure, but directly at the lethal spike plate—subverting the player's blind obedience!

### 2.3 Bilu / Ogre (ဘီလူး) & Shadow Logic
* **Archetype:** Giant brute strength, easily enraged, terrified of reflections and sudden loud gongs.
* **Key Puzzle Applications:**
  - **The Bronze Gong Resonance (မောင်းသံတုန်ခါမှု):** Striking a temple gong with a heavy stone creates sound vibrations that shatter brittle crystal barriers or temporarily stun stone guardians.
  - **Shadowless Test (အရိပ်မထွက်သော ဘီလူး):** Identifying false stone duplicates or secret illusions by watching where light sources and ceiling torches cast shadows.

## 3. Vintage Satire, Burmese Comics & Street Culture

### 3.1 U Shwe Yoe & Daw Moe (ဦးရွှေရိုးနှင့် ဒေါ်မိုး)
* **Archetype:** High-energy slapstick comedy, eccentric dance steps, loose longyi malfunctions.
* **Key Mechanics:**
  - **Slipping on Wet Longyi:** Rapid belly-sliding across frictionless wet pagoda tiles under low saw blades.
  - **Umbrella Float / Parachute:** Opening a traditional oiled-paper Pathein umbrella (*ပုသိမ်ထီး*) to glide gently across wide chasms or slow down a fatal drop.

### 3.2 Satirical Cartoons: Pe Thein & Ba Gyan (ကာတွန်း ဦးဖေသိန်း၊ ဦးဘဂျမ်း)
* **Archetype:** Rural village absurdism, slapstick contraptions, and hilarious over-engineering (*စက်ပစ္စည်းဆန်းများ*).
* **Key Mechanics:**
  - **Rube Goldberg Watermill:** Channeling canal water through hollow bamboo pipes, tipping coconut shells to drop a weight onto a rusty rusted lever.
  - **The Slingshot Coconut:** Using elastic longyi fabric wrapped around two teak pillars to launch stones across the arena.

### 3.3 Detective U San Shar (စုံထောက် ဦးစံရှား)
* **Archetype:** Colonial Burmese Sherlock Holmes (Shwe U Daung's legendary detective).
* **Key Mechanics:**
  - **Cigar Ash & Footprint Trail:** Revealing the safe walkable path on a booby-trapped tile floor by blowing cheroot smoke or dropping betel dust.
  - **Clockwork Gear Jamming:** Slipping a heavy iron hairpin or betel cutter between brass teeth to freeze an ancient mechanism.

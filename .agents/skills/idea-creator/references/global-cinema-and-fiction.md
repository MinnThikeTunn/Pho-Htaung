## 2. Slapstick Comedy & Cartoon Animation

### 2.1 Home Alone (Chris Columbus / John Hughes)
* **Micro-Machines on Hardwood (Friction Zero Trap):**
  - *Film Scene:* Marv and Harry step on dozens of toy cars and crash violently onto their backs.
  - *Game Mechanic:* Dropping round betel nuts or smooth river pebbles onto a smooth stone slope. Enemies or heavy rolling boulders slip and crash out of control.
* **The Flaming Doorknob / Electrified Handle:**
  - *Film Scene:* Harry grabs a red-hot doorknob and burns the letter "M" into his palm.
  - *Game Mechanic:* A lever heated by a nearby oil furnace. Grabbing it bare-handed causes Phoe Htaung to leap into the air waving scorched hands; wrapping longyi cloth around hands allows safe interaction.
* **The Pendulum Paint Can to the Face:**
  - *Film Scene:* Heavy paint cans swinging on strings from the top of the stairwell.
  - *Game Mechanic:* A swinging clay pot or bronze weight. The first hit delivers the **Slapstick Cushion** (squashing Phoe Htaung with cartoon birds circling his head without death count increment).

### 2.2 Buster Keaton & Charlie Chaplin (Silent Cinema Stunts)
* **The Falling House Facade (*Steamboat Bill, Jr.*):**
  - *Cinema Scene:* An entire two-story house wall falls directly over Buster Keaton; he emerges unharmed because he was standing precisely where the attic window was.
  - *Game Mechanic:* An entire stone wall collapses forward across the screen. Phoe Htaung survives only if standing in the exact slot where an archway or window aperture aligns with his coordinates!
* **The Clock Tower Hands (*Safety Last!* - Harold Lloyd):**
  - *Cinema Scene:* Dangling desperately from the minute hand of a giant clock as it slowly ticks downward.
  - *Game Mechanic:* Hanging onto a giant gear tooth or pendulum needle while waiting for a platform below to align.

### 2.3 Looney Tunes & Merrie Melodies (Chuck Jones / Tex Avery)
